Pixel Studio · scene library

Eleven scenes, four ways each

Each picture is painted by the Studio from a handful of settings, at 320×180 to 960×540 pixels and scaled up with crisp edges (or smoothly, for the HD variants). Every scene has a baseline with fairly default settings, then three variants that change the light, the weather, the season or what is in the scene. Under each one you’ll find the prompt that makes it and the settings it uses. Tap a picture to play it live. The three newest scenes vary the camera and the style: close-ups, low and high angles, slow pans, and the Pixel ↔ HD slider from crisp pixels to smooth, painted HD.

Clouds drift, plants and people sway, water glints and lamps flicker. In the Studio, settings in 1–6 go from the scene itself to the fine detail.

In each recipe, settings in gold differ from that scene’s baseline. Anything not listed stays at its default.

Where the Studio started

first version

The first eight presets, all countryside, painted by the earliest Studio build. They led to the scene types above.

The Studio is one self-contained file in the project, studio/pixel-studio.html. Opened on its own (use Open the Studio) it also saves PNGs at 1080p and 4K and records 10-second videos.
